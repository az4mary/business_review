### **Key Optimizations Made**

* **Clear Responsibility Boundaries:** The initial lawn care is kept in Phase 1 to establish the baseline, allowing you to clearly pass ongoing maintenance to the tenant for the remainder of the 36 months, protecting your cash flow.
* **Pest Control Cadence:** Replaced the one-time April 2027 pest control with an ongoing quarterly schedule. Consistent pest control is relatively cheap and prevents catastrophic infestations that could derail your strictly cash-flow-based budget.
* **Strategic Stalling:** Insulating the subfloor is neatly separated from repairing the subfloor. Fixing it secures the safety liability immediately (Phase 2), while the insulation is pushed out to Phase 3 to allow the rental income to build back up.
