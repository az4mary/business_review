# ZYNE Homepage Project Requirements Document

## 1. Project Overview

### Project Name

ZYNE Homepage Build

### Objective

Create a premium, conversion-focused homepage for ZYNE that functions as the primary sales and routing layer for ZYNE’s paid productized services.

The homepage must communicate ZYNE’s premium positioning, segment visitors by buyer intent, present fixed-price service options, and route buyers either to relevant internal service/category pages or directly to exact Stan Store checkout links where appropriate.

### Business Model Requirement

ZYNE does not offer free discovery calls or free consultations.

All products and services are paid offers. The ZYNE website must provide complete product education and conversion content. Stan Store is used only as the checkout destination after the buyer clicks a purchase CTA.

### Core Conversion Principle

The homepage should not optimize for “contact us.” It should optimize for:

1. Buyer self-diagnosis
2. Product discovery
3. Paid-offer comparison
4. Purchase intent
5. Redirect to the exact Stan Store checkout product

---

## 2. Primary Goals

### Conversion Goals

The homepage must:

* Establish ZYNE as a premium growth intelligence and execution studio.
* Make clear that services are paid, productized offers.
* Route visitors into one of five buyer-intent paths:

  * Grow My Visibility
  * Build My Brand
  * Improve My Business
  * Use AI
  * Convert More Clients
* Highlight low-friction starter products.
* Highlight premium strategic products.
* Allow buyers to review product details before checkout.
* Send buyers to the exact Stan Store checkout URL when they click a final “Buy Now” or “Checkout on Stan Store” CTA.
* Avoid any language that implies a free call or free consultation.

### SEO Goals

The homepage must:

* Use crawlable HTML text for all key headings, service names, product names, and category descriptions.
* Support internal linking to category and product detail pages.
* Include keyword-relevant language for:

  * Google Business Profile optimization
  * local visibility services
  * brand identity services
  * website conversion services
  * AI automation services
  * AI chatbot systems
  * business growth strategy
  * market positioning
  * client conversion systems
* Support future structured data implementation for services, offers, breadcrumbs, and FAQs.

### UX Goals

The homepage must:

* Feel premium, authoritative, and clear.
* Reduce decision friction.
* Avoid overwhelming buyers with the full catalog at once.
* Use comparison-oriented sections where product ladders exist.
* Be fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile.
* Make checkout redirection clear before the user leaves the ZYNE site.

## 4. Required Site Behavior

### Checkout Behavior

The ZYNE website is the sales and education layer.

Stan Store is the payment and checkout layer.

CTA behavior must follow this model:

| CTA Type               | Destination                                           |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Shop Services          | Internal service index or product catalog page        |
| Find My Growth Path    | Internal homepage section anchor or category selector |
| View Product           | Internal product detail page                          |
| View Category          | Internal category page                                |
| Buy Now                | Exact Stan Store checkout product URL                 |
| Checkout on Stan Store | Exact Stan Store checkout product URL                 |

### Required Redirect Disclosure

Before any user is redirected to Stan Store, the page should clearly communicate:

“Secure checkout is completed through Stan Store.”

This can appear:

* Under buy buttons
* Inside product cards
* In product detail sections
* In the final CTA block

### Prohibited CTA Language

Do not use:

* Schedule a Free Call
* Book a Free Consultation
* Get a Free Strategy Session
* Contact Us for Pricing
* Talk to Sales
* Request a Free Audit

### Allowed CTA Language

Use:

* Shop Paid Services
* View Product
* View Service Details
* Buy Now
* Checkout on Stan Store
* Purchase This Service
* View Executive Briefing — $750
* Find My Growth Path
* Explore Growth Services

## 9. Accessibility Requirements

The homepage must meet baseline WCAG 2.1 AA expectations.

Required:

* Sufficient color contrast between gold text and black background.
* Keyboard-accessible navigation.
* Visible focus states.
* Alt text for meaningful images.
* Decorative images marked appropriately.
* Buttons and links must have descriptive labels.
* No text embedded inside important imagery.
* Mobile tap targets should be large enough for touch interaction.
* Cards must be navigable by keyboard if clickable.

---

## 10. Performance Requirements

The homepage must be optimized for speed despite premium visuals.

Required:

* Use optimized image formats such as WebP or AVIF.
* Lazy-load below-the-fold images.
* Avoid large uncompressed background images.
* Avoid excessive animation.
* Use SVG icons where possible.
* Ensure hero image is optimized and preloaded if necessary.
* Minimize third-party scripts.
* Target Lighthouse Performance score of 85+ on mobile and 90+ on desktop where feasible.

## 18. Developer Notes

### Critical Implementation Note

The homepage is not a brochure and not a lead-generation page. It is a premium productized-services storefront.

The user journey should move from:

Problem → Category → Product → Details → Buy Now → Stan Store checkout

not:

Problem → Contact form → Free call

### Required External Links

Stan Store checkout links will be provided separately. Until then, use placeholder values in product data:

`stanCheckoutUrl: "TO_BE_PROVIDED"`

Do not use dummy public URLs in production.

### Internal Routes to Prepare

At minimum, the homepage should link to these planned internal routes:

```txt
/services/
/grow-my-visibility/
/build-my-brand/
/improve-my-business/
/use-ai/
/convert-more-clients/
/intelligence/
/delivery/
```

```txt

```

/services/starter-brand-kit/
