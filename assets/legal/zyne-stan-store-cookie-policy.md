## 1. What Cookies and Similar Technologies Are

Cookies are small files placed on a device or browser when a website is visited. Similar technologies may include pixels, tags, scripts, local storage, session storage, software development kits, tracking links, analytics identifiers, and device identifiers.

These technologies can help websites function, remember preferences, measure performance, understand visitor behavior, support security, and evaluate marketing or conversion activity.

ZYNE may use cookies and similar technologies for:

* essential website functionality;

* analytics and product-interest measurement;
* product-card and growth-path interaction tracking;
* Stan Store redirect tracking;
* conversion-flow measurement;
* advertising or retargeting, if enabled;
* remembering browser or user-experience preferences.
